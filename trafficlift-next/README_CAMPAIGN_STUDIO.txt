TRAFFICLIFT PRO 5.2.2 — CAMPAIGN STUDIO

INSTALL
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Double-click Install_TrafficLift_Pro_5.bat.
3. Wait for the local voice/video check and the ready message.
4. Use the NEW TrafficLift Pro 5 desktop shortcut.

The installer locates your old TrafficLift Studio shortcut, then installs a separate copy under LocalAppData. It shares your existing Python/video runtime and voice models without modifying them. It creates no paid AI calls. Old files and shortcut are preserved. Port 8005 is used for the new studio; the old studio uses 8000. Keep the original runtime folder because version 5 depends on its voice/video tools. The installer copies your local .env settings privately on your PC; no .env or credentials are included in this ZIP. Windows installer execution itself could not be verified in this Linux workspace; the installation gate checks real local rendering before creating the new shortcut.

YOUR FIRST CAMPAIGN
- Enter your business name, selling website, approved research websites and target platforms in My setup.
- For Amazon affiliate selling, choose affiliate mode and amazon.com as the selling website. Save your own Associates tag to create the exact product purchase link and start the kit automatically after research. Without a saved tag, paste the full affiliate product URL for each campaign; shortened links are not supported.
- For your own shop or Gumroad, enter the actual storefront domain. A store homepage is not a product purchase destination.
- Optionally enter product topics for your audience, such as kitchen tools or printable planners. Gumroad-only research defaults to digital-product topics.
- Find my next product searches ONLY approved domains and requires buying-related source signals plus a usable photo. Connect your Tavily key if needed. No live research request was made in our tests.
- Use my product link analyzes one exact listing without replacing it. Import does not verify current sales demand.
- For your own exact listing on a non-shared store domain or an Amazon affiliate product with your saved tag, the kit starts automatically. Other products need the exact purchase page and confirmation that it sells the selected product. A Target or other research link is never substituted for your chosen store.
- Create my marketing kit creates full-photo images and a narrated photo-based short video locally. Defaults are 15 seconds and Clean style; 30 and 60 seconds are available.
- Download complete kit includes pin 1000x1500, square image 1080x1080, story image 1080x1920, unbranded source photo, video, scripts, six platform guides and dated research evidence where available.
- Open a saved kit without rebuilding. Edit/save captions. Plan a manual posting date, download calendar reminders, record uploaded post links and enter results from your dashboards.

PLATFORM HANDLING
Pinterest: title, description, pin, purchase destination and board instructions.
TikTok/Instagram/YouTube Shorts: vertical social video and editable caption. Use a profile/eligible platform link route; YouTube Shorts description links are not clickable.
Amazon: unbranded product photo and listing copy for a listing you are authorized to edit. The social video is NOT an Amazon listing-video export or a product compliance certification.
Gumroad: supporting artwork, promotional video and description for an existing product listing. The app does not create a new checkout product automatically.
Owned-store links get channel-specific UTM tags. Affiliate URLs are preserved unchanged and disclosure copy is included for social promotion.

WHAT THIS BUILD DOES NOT CLAIM
No verified real-time internet-wide purchases, guaranteed winning products, profits or organic traffic. Research retrieval times are not publication times or confirmed transactions. No direct account posting, background auto-publication, automatic platform analytics, AI avatars or generated filmed demonstrations. The app cannot guarantee that research-discovered products are already stocked or authorized for sale on your own store; you must supply the matching purchase page when it is not your own verified source listing. Planned dates are reminders, not scheduled API publishing. Results entered by users are labeled user-reported. TikTok/Meta/Pinterest/YouTube authorization and developer approval remain external setup requirements for direct integrations. This is a tested campaign-workflow upgrade, not complete Zeely feature parity.
